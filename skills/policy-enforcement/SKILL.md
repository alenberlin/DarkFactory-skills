# Policy Enforcement

Use the active schema-valid policy and its exact identity. Advisory reports quality violations; Enforced blocks mandatory quality gates; Strict also requires declared isolation and capability admission. Authorization boundaries hold in every mode. Background scheduling defaults to Strict and cannot silently downgrade.

Map each engineering rule to a protected executable check, evidence condition or independent judgment review. Reports include failed prerequisites, policy ID, source identity and a concrete remediation. Unknown or stale evidence cannot pass. A model’s success claim has no authority.

Agents may propose policy changes but cannot activate them, write authoritative state, approve themselves, weaken required tests or delete audit history. Administrative policy/blueprint changes require review and renewed approval. Distinguish proven effect isolation from promises expressed only in Markdown. Unsupported capabilities remain visible and block the corresponding operation.

Read [the runtime contract](../../docs/RUNTIME_CONTRACT.md) for proposal and authority boundaries and [the operating guide](../../docs/AUTOPILOT.md) for actual commands and compatibility limits.
